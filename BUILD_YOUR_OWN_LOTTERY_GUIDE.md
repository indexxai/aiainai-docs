# Build Your Own Lottery — User Guide

## What Is "Build Your Own Lottery"?

A **Lottery** is your personal prediction ticket. Instead of playing a fixed lottery draw where the events are pre-selected by an admin, **Build Your Own Lottery** lets you hand-pick exactly which 7 real-world events you want to predict — from a pool of 20+ live prediction markets across categories like Crypto, Sports, Finance, Politics, Technology, and more.

You still win prizes the same way: match 4 or more of your predictions correctly to win a share of the pool. Match all 7 to win or share the **Grand Jackpot**.

## How It Works — Step by Step

---

### Step 1: Choose Your 7 Events

> Navigate to `/slate/build` to start the wizard. You land on the **Browse Events** screen.

![Step 1 — Browse Events](public/docs/lottery-step2-browse.png)

**What you see:**
- A **3-step progress bar** at the top (Choose 7 Events → Make Predictions → Confirm & Enter)
- **Category filter pills**: All, Climate, Crypto, Culture, Economy, Finance, Geopolitics, Politics, Sports, Technology
- A **grid of 20 event cards**, each showing:
  - The event **category** badge
  - A "**Crowd says X% → Yes/No**" indicator showing current consensus
  - The **question** being predicted
  - A **+ button** on the right to add the event

**How to add an event:**
- Tap the **+** button on any event card. It highlights in violet and the + turns into ×.
- A **cart rail** appears at the bottom showing your progress (`0 / 7 events selected`) and chip previews of selected events.
- Keep picking until you have 7.

---

#### After selecting all 7 events:

![Step 1 — 7 Events Selected](public/docs/lottery-step2-selected.png)

**What changes:**
- The bottom rail shows **"7 / 7 events selected"** with a violet **Next →** button
- Chips at the bottom preview all your chosen events
- All unselected cards grey out (you've reached the 7-event limit)
- Tap **×** on any chip to swap it out for a different event

**When ready:** Tap **Next →** to proceed to Step 2.

---

### Step 2: Make Your Predictions

> After tapping Next, you arrive at the **Make Predictions** screen with your 7 chosen events.

![Step 2 — Pick Predictions](public/docs/lottery-step3-predictions.png)

**What you see:**
- The **progress bar** advances to Step 2
- A hint: *"For each event, pick the outcome you think will happen"*
- Your 7 events as cards, each showing:
  - Event number (1–7) and category badge
  - The question
  - **Outcome buttons** (Yes/No or team names) with a **"Crowd says X%"** probability bar

**How to pick:**
- Tap the outcome you believe will happen — it highlights violet with a checkmark.
- The **probability bar** shows the current crowd consensus. Use it as a guide, but pick what *you* believe.
- To change your mind, tap a different outcome. To undo, tap the same one again.

---

#### After picking all 7 outcomes:

![Step 2 — All Predictions Made](public/docs/lottery-step3-filled.png)

**What changes:**
- Each card shows **"You picked [outcome]"** in violet
- All **7 progress dots** at the top fill violet
- The bottom bar shows *"All predictions made! Review your ticket →"*
- The **Review** button unlocks

**When ready:** Tap **Review** to go to Step 3.

---

### Step 3: Confirm & Enter

> The final screen shows a full summary before you pay and lock in your ticket.

![Step 3 — Confirm & Enter](public/docs/lottery-step4-confirm.png)

**What you see:**

1. **Grand Jackpot banner** — the prize for matching all 7 correctly (e.g. $50,000.00)

2. **Your Predictions** — a numbered list of all 7 events with your chosen outcome shown as a violet badge

3. **Number of Tickets** — choose how many tickets to buy:

   | Tickets | Tag    | Effect |
   |---------|--------|--------|
   | 1       | Basic  | Standard entry at base price |
   | 2       | Double | 2× entry fee, 2× potential winnings |
   | 5       | Boost  | 5× entry fee, 5× potential winnings |
   | 10      | Max    | 10× entry fee, 10× potential winnings |

4. **Prize Tiers** — how many correct predictions to win:

   | Matches | Prize |
   |---------|-------|
   | Match 4 | Share of pool (5%) |
   | Match 5 | Share of pool (10%) |
   | Match 6 | Share of pool (20%) |
   | Grand Jackpot (7/7) | Full jackpot share (65%) |

5. **Total entry cost** displayed at the bottom (e.g. $5.00 for 1 ticket)

6. A note showing when the current draw closes

**To submit:**
- Tap **Enter — $X.XX** (button shows your exact total cost)
- If not signed in, you will be prompted to log in first
- Once entered, your picks are **locked** — they cannot be changed
- You are redirected to your **Ticket Tracking page** to follow results live

## Tips for Building a Strong Lottery

- **Mix categories** — spreading across Crypto, Sports, Finance, and Politics reduces correlated risk. If one category goes badly, others may still pay off.
- **Check "Crowd says %"** — when the crowd is heavily one-sided (e.g. 80%+), a contrarian pick is higher risk but pays off more meaningfully if you're right.
- **Pick events you actually follow** — your edge comes from domain knowledge, not random guessing.
- **Start with 1 ticket** on your first lottery to learn the feel, then boost with more tickets once you're confident.
- **Avoid events closing very soon** — events with less than 1 hour until cutoff are marked with an amber flashing timer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I enter the same draw multiple times with different lotteries?**
Yes. Each "Build Your Own" submission creates a separate ticket. Your tickets are tracked independently.

**What happens if one of my events gets voided?**
If an event is voided, that pick is excluded from the match count. A 7-event lottery with one voided pick is scored out of 6.

**Is "Build Your Own Lottery" the same as the featured curated draws?**
They use the same jackpot and prize pool, but in a curated draw the 7 events are selected by the platform admin. In "Build Your Own", you choose the events yourself. Both follow the same prize tier rules.

**When do I find out if I won?**
Results update in real time on your Ticket Tracking page as each event resolves. Your final match count and payout are confirmed once all 7 events have resolved.

**Where do I see my tickets?**
Go to **My Tickets** (`/slate/tickets`). All active and past tickets — both curated draw tickets and custom lottery tickets — appear there.
